lore: the console owns the viewport, so the chat header stops scrolling away

The shell grew with its content: a long conversation, or a long list of recent
chats in the sidebar, made the whole page scroll and took the chat header with
it. The shell is now h-screen with overflow-hidden, the sidebar scrolls its own
chat list, and only the main content scrolls. The chat view fills its slot
instead of measuring the window, and its transcript is the only scroll area
under a fixed header.
