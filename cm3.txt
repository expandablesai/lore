lore: every message says when it was said, and copies in one click

- appendMessage returns the stored timestamp, and /chat echoes both the question
  and the answer stamp, so a live turn is labelled exactly like a reloaded one
- the transcript shows "<day> at <time>" under each message, localised through
  the interface language (date and time formatted by Intl, joined by a
  translated connector)
- a copy button under every message writes the text to the clipboard, with a
  selection-based fallback for browsers without the clipboard API, and a short
  "Copied" confirmation
