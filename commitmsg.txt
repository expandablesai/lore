lore: memory that survives a new chat

- first-person facts the reader states (my name is DK, remember that ...) are
  captured as confirmed personal assertions with the chat turn as their source,
  and every later turn loads them into the prompt
- related turns from the reader's other conversations are folded in, so a
  discussion can be picked up in a fresh thread
- document retrieval now takes at most two passages per document, so a long
  screenplay or a pile of image descriptions cannot crowd out a one-line note
  that answers the question
- the empty-answer retry keeps the profile block instead of dropping it
