# 05 — Naming and Trademark

> You own **EXPANDABLES**. That is the asset; everything else is a sub-brand decision.
> This is product/brand reasoning, not legal advice — clearance and filings need an IP professional.

---

## 1. First, an honest critique of "EXPANDABLES OS"

**What works:** it signals a platform, it is memorable, and it sits naturally on top of a family of
components. As an *umbrella* it is fine.

**What does not work:**

| Problem | Detail |
|---|---|
| **"OS" is a category word** | Weak as a trademark element on its own, and it promises an operating system. Users do not want an OS; they want a layer that makes their existing tools smarter |
| **"Memory OS" is taken as a descriptor** | MemOS published *"MemOS: A Memory OS for AI System"* ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03724)) — you would be fighting an academic and, soon, product collision on the exact phrase |
| **It front-loads the commodity** | "OS" says infrastructure. The differentiator is *trust and recall quality*, not an operating system |
| **It ages badly** | If the product becomes a vault + compiler, "OS" is an anchor dragging behind it |

**Recommendation:** keep **EXPANDABLES OS** as the platform/company umbrella, and give the product a real
name that can carry a logo, a domain and a filing.

## 2. Shortlist

Scored 1–5 on: distinctiveness, fit, sayability, extensibility (sub-brands), collision risk (5 = low risk).

| Name | Distinctive | Fit | Sayable | Extensible | Low collision | Total | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EXPANDABLES CORTEX** | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 3 | **22** | Brain-adjacent, short, tech-neutral. "Cortex" alone is crowded, but the composite is yours |
| **EXPANDABLES VAULT** | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 19 | Strong trust signal; collides conceptually with HashiCorp Vault |
| **EXPANDABLES LORE** | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **22** | "Your project's lore" — memorable, developer-native, story-rich. Slightly informal for enterprise |
| **EXPANDABLES CONTINUUM** | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 20 | Memory across time; long to say |
| **EXPANDABLES LEDGER** | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 19 | Provenance/audit angle; finance connotation |
| **EXPANDABLES ATLAS** | 2 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 18 | Too crowded (MongoDB Atlas, Atlas Copco…) |
| **EXPANDABLES ECHO** | 2 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 1 | 15 | Amazon Echo; also "echo" = repetition/hallucination. Avoid |
| **EXPANDABLES MEMORY** | 1 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 21 | Safest legal footing, weakest brand. Fine as a *descriptor* ("EXPANDABLES Memory") |
| **EXPANDABLES SUBSTRATE** | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 19 | Very technical, very credible to engineers, cold for everyone else |

### Decision taken (2026-10-29): **`EXPANDABLES LORE`**

You chose LORE. It is the stronger *product and community* name: short, sayable, memorable, and it says
something true — a project has lore: constraints, decisions, scars, received wisdom. It also fits an
open-source project in a way CORTEX never would (CORTEX reads like a vendor module).

```
EXPANDABLES OS ............. platform umbrella (company / platform)
└── EXPANDABLES LORE ....... the memory vault + compiler (the product)
    ├── LORE LINK .......... capture: MCP, proxy, SDKs, webhooks
    ├── LORE LENS .......... browser extension (phase 2)
    ├── LORE COMPASS ....... decisions + consistency checker
    ├── LORE GUARD ......... provenance, poisoning defense, audit
    └── LORE CONSOLE ....... the memory browser UI
```

`EXPANDABLES CORTEX` stays on the shelf as a fallback if enterprise buyers push back on "lore" as too
informal — that is a rebrand of a product, not of the company, which is survivable.

**Watch-outs specific to LORE:**
- "Lore" is a common English word — the composite `EXPANDABLES LORE` is what you protect, never "Lore" alone.
- Check for collision with tabletop/RPG and "lorebook" AI tooling (there is a genre of `lorebook` tools in
  the roleplay-AI space); be explicit in copy that this is project memory, not character lore.
- Domain plan: `expandableslore.com`, `lore.expandables.dev`, `get.expandables.dev/lore` for the installer.
- GitHub org `expandables`, repo `lore`; npm `@expandables/lore`, PyPI `expandables-lore`.

## 3. Names to actively avoid

- Anything containing **"Memory OS"** — academic prior use and descriptive weakness.
- **"OpenMemory"**, **"Mem0"**, **"Zep"**-adjacent constructions — confusion with funded incumbents.
- Vendor names in the mark (**GPT**, **Claude**, **Copilot**, **ChatGPT**) — never in a trademark; only in
  nominative descriptive text ("works with Claude"), and even then, check each vendor's brand guidelines.
- **"Passport"** as the mark — Mem0 already uses "memory passport" in its positioning; use it only as a
  feature description.

## 4. Trademark action list (do before printing anything)

1. **Confirm what EXPANDABLES actually covers.** Pull your registration certificate: which classes
   (9 = software, 42 = SaaS/technical services), which jurisdictions, which goods/services wording.
   A registration in an unrelated class does **not** cover a memory product.
2. **File the composite** `EXPANDABLES CORTEX` (word mark) in class 9 and 42 in your primary markets
   (IN, US, EU at minimum), intent-to-use where needed. Composite marks are examined as a whole, which is
   why the suffix helps.
3. **Clearance search** for "Cortex" in classes 9/42 in your markets — expect crowding; the question is
   whether *confusion* is likely, not whether the word exists elsewhere.
4. **Secure the namespace** before announcing: `expandables.ai`, `expandablescortex.com`,
   `cortex.expandables.*`, GitHub org, npm scope (`@expandables/*`), PyPI, X/GitHub handles.
5. **Watch the "OS" usage.** Use "EXPANDABLES OS" as a platform descriptor in body copy rather than as the
   primary mark; never as "ExpandablesOS™" without checking how your registration reads.
6. **Brand guideline rule:** product = CORTEX, company = EXPANDABLES, category = "memory vault" /
   "persistent memory layer". Consistent use is what makes a mark enforceable.

---

## 5. Naming tests applied

- **Radio test:** "Expandables Cortex" — unambiguous, spellable after one hearing.
- **Bar test:** explains itself in one line: *"It's the memory your AI tools share."*
- **Extension test:** CORTEX LINK / LENS / GUARD all read naturally — no strained metaphors.
- **International check:** "cortex" is Latin-derived and neutral in EN/DE/FR/ES/PT; verify JP/KR/CN
  transliterations before localisation.
- **Collision check:** unresolved until a professional search runs — treat the table above as a
  starting hypothesis, not clearance.
